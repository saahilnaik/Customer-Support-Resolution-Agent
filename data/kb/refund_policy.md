# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Orders can be refunded within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused or defective.
Orders delivered more than 30 days ago are not eligible for a refund, but may qualify for a warranty claim.

## Refund amounts
Full refunds are given for defective, damaged or wrong items. Partial refunds may be offered for items returned in used condition.
Shipping fees are refunded only when the item arrived damaged or was not as described.

## Approval rules
Refunds up to $50 can be issued immediately by the support agent. Refunds above $50 require approval from a human support manager.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
