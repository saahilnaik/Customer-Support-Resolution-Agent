# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Orders are processed within 24 hours.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once the order ships. Orders in "processing" status have not shipped yet and can still be cancelled or have their shipping address changed.

## Lost or delayed packages
If a package has not arrived 10 days after shipping, contact support. We will either re-ship the item or issue a full refund.

## Cancellation
Orders in processing status can be cancelled in the chat right away, with a full refund of the amount paid. Orders that have shipped but not yet been delivered can sometimes be intercepted with the carrier; these cancellations are reviewed by a manager before they are confirmed. Delivered orders cannot be cancelled and must be returned instead (see the refund policy).
