# Warranty

## Coverage
All electronics carry a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage and water damage are not covered.

## Claims
To make a warranty claim, register a complaint with the order number and a description of the defect. A replacement or repair is offered; a refund is issued if no replacement is available.

## Gold tier
Gold-tier customers receive a 24-month warranty and priority handling of all complaints.
